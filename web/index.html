Open any line and you get the Devanagari and the chapter-and-verse. Where a citation could not be checked against a printed edition, the platform says so instead of implying authority it does not have.

Questions

The things people ask before they trust a reading

Why is there no death date?

The classical longevity arithmetic is disputed by the tradition itself. Phaladīpikā 22.24 records that Bhadanta rejected the piṇḍāyu as unsound, and that Varāhamihira said the same. The haraṇa reductions are therefore not computed. A figure from a disputed method would be worse than no figure.

Why does it sometimes refuse to answer?

Phaladīpikā 15.6 states that two or three conditions must agree before a severe loss is declared. Where they do not, the engine withholds the reading and says that it is withholding it. That is the text's instruction, not caution layered over it.

Where do the verses come from?

Ninety-one verses of Phaladīpikā across chapters 6, 7, 9, 10, 16, 19, 22 and 26, checked against Mantreśvara, Phaladeepika, translated by P. V. Subrahmanya Sastri, second edition, Munshiram Manoharlal, Delhi, 1950.

Is my birth data stored?

No. The chart is computed in your browser and never sent anywhere. There is no account, no database and no server-side calculation.
