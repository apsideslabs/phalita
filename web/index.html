Ten chapters, one for each part of a life

Each chapter is written from the houses that govern it, and says what the chart gives, what it costs, when it activates, and what to do about it.

Work and standing

the tenth, sixth and eleventh

What you do in public, how far it carries you, and whether the chart points to service or to your own enterprise.

Money

the second and eleventh

What comes in, what stays, where the leak is — and whether the chart rewards a big bet or a slow build.

Marriage and partnership

the seventh and second

Whether the bond settles or strains, and whether the chart reads a love match or an arranged one.

Children and creative work

the fifth and ninth

What you bring into the world and pass on — children, students, or the work you make rather than the work you do.

Home and property

the fourth

The ground you stand on: property, vehicles, domestic peace, and whether they accumulate or have to be built.

Body and health

the first and sixth

Constitution and recovery, the recurring weak point, and how much of your energy has to be managed rather than spent.

Mind and temperament

the first, fourth and fifth

How you think under pressure, where the restlessness comes from, and what steadies it.

Fortune and support

the ninth and eleventh

Help that arrives from outside your own effort — teachers, elders, and the doors that open without pushing.

Family and siblings

the third and fourth

The people you came from, and whether they carry you or whether you build your own ground.

Dharma and inward life

the ninth and twelfth

The pull towards what lies behind things, and whether solitude rests you or costs you.

Money the second and eleventh

Money is one of the well-placed parts of this chart. It accumulates, and it accumulates from things you already hold rather than from windfalls.

You keep what you earn; your word carries weight in money matters and you see a financial situation before others do.

The leak is worth naming anyway. Money leaves through obligations rather than through spending, and family resources do not back you when you need them.

This chapter is most active during the Jupiter period, 2020 to 2036 — that is when what it describes is most likely to be happening.

Build slowly and keep what you build. This is not a chart that rewards a big bet.

Every sentence above resolves to a verse. Open the reading and each chapter carries its Devanagari and its chapter-and-verse.

Four steps, no guessing

1

Birth data

Date, time and place. Nothing else is asked for.

2

The chart

Sidereal positions, houses, dignities, strengths and the running daśā are computed from the ephemeris.

3

Conditions tested

Every rule the text states is tested against that chart. A rule that cannot fire is not in the corpus.

4

Written out

What fires becomes prose. What contradicts itself is shown as contradicting itself, not quietly resolved.

What it will not do

No death date

The classical arithmetic is disputed by the tradition itself. 22.24 records that Bhadanta rejected the piṇḍāyu and Varāhamihira agreed. A figure from a disputed method is worse than none.

No verdict from one condition

Phaladīpikā 15.6 states that two or three conditions must agree before a severe loss is declared. Where they do not, the engine says it is withholding the reading.

No chart drawing

That is what a calculation platform is for. This one answers in words.
