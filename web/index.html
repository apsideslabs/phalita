The reading

Ten chapters, written rather than tabulated

Most engines hand you a list of placements and leave you to work out what they mean. This one commits to a judgement and says it in a sentence.

  • Work and standing — the tenth, sixth and eleventh
  • Money — the second and eleventh
  • Marriage and partnership — the seventh and second
  • Children and creative work — the fifth and ninth
  • Home, body, mind, fortune, family, dharma
Moneythe second and eleventh

Money is one of the well-placed parts of this chart. It accumulates, and it accumulates from things you already hold rather than from windfalls.

You keep what you earn; your word carries weight in money matters and you see a financial situation before others do.

The leak is worth naming anyway. Money leaves through obligations rather than through spending.

This chapter is most active during the Jupiter period, 2020 to 2036.

Provenance

Every sentence opens onto its verse

Under each statement sits the citation. Open it and you get the Devanagari and the chapter-and-verse. Where a citation could not be checked against a printed edition, the platform says so instead of implying authority it does not have.

  • 91 verses across eight chapters of Phaladīpikā
  • Checked page by page against the 1950 printed edition
  • Textual disputes kept as disputes, not resolved quietly
Phaladīpikā 10.15adhyāya 10 · śloka 15

कलत्रनाथे रिपुनीचसंस्थे मूढेऽथवा पापनिरीक्षिते वा ।
कलत्रभे पापयुतेऽथ दृष्टे कलत्रहानीं प्रवदन्ति सन्तः ॥

The verse names a configuration, not a single clause — which is why the engine withholds it when only one condition holds.

Method

Four steps, and no guessing between them

Nothing in the reading is produced by a model improvising. Every line traces back to a condition the text states and the engine tests.

  • Birth data in — date, time, place
  • Sidereal chart computed: positions, houses, dignities, strengths, daśā
  • Every rule tested; a rule that cannot fire is not in the corpus
  • What fires becomes prose, with its period attached

Questions

Why is there no death date?

The classical longevity arithmetic is disputed by the tradition itself. Phaladīpikā 22.24 records that Bhadanta rejected the piṇḍāyu as unsound, and that Varāhamihira said the same. The haraṇa reductions are therefore not computed. A figure from a disputed method would be worse than no figure.

Why does it sometimes refuse to answer?

Phaladīpikā 15.6 states that two or three conditions must agree before a severe loss is declared. Where they do not, the engine withholds the reading and says that it is withholding it. That is the text's instruction, not caution layered over it.

Where do the verses come from?

Ninety-one verses of Phaladīpikā across chapters 6, 7, 9, 10, 16, 19, 22 and 26, checked against Mantreśvara, Phaladeepika, translated by P. V. Subrahmanya Sastri, second edition, Munshiram Manoharlal, Delhi, 1950.

Is my birth data stored?

No. The chart is computed in your browser and never sent anywhere. There is no account, no database and no server-side calculation.
