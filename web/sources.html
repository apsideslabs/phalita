The texts behind the reading

What is quoted, what has been checked, and what was taken out.

Phaladīpikā

Of Mantreśvara. Chapters 6, 7, 9, 10, 16, 19, 22 and 26 are transcribed — ninety-one verses. The printed edition used for checking is Mantreśvara, Phaladeepika, adhyāyas I–XXVIII, translated by P. V. Subrahmanya Sastri, second edition, Munshiram Manoharlal, Delhi, 1950.

Chapter X, on marriage, was checked page by page against that edition. It confirmed verse 15 verbatim, and it caught an error in this engine: verse 13 was being used as a cancellation of the harsh reading when it is in fact a statement about when a marriage happens. That is now fixed.

Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra

Volume I, translated by R. Santhanam. Used to cross-check the method, and for the dispute over the seventh lord in the fifth house: the verse there reads favourably — honourable, virtuous, delighted — while Santhanam's own notes record the opposite in practice, delay and disappointment in married life and affliction to children. Both are kept. The tradition reads that placement two ways, and so does this engine.

Bṛhat Jātaka

Of Varāhamihira, translated by P. S. Sastri. Used for the nakṣatra material and for cross-checking the aspect scheme. Its Devanagari is corrupt in the available scan, so nothing is quoted from it. Note also that Varāhamihira has no Rāhu, no Ketu and no Viṃśottarī — a different school, and a real disagreement with the Parāśara tradition rather than a variant of it.

What was withdrawn

A twelve-domain panel of twenty-three rules once stood on this platform. Its first rule cited Phaladīpikā chapter 14 for a verse that is not in it — chapter 14 is रोगनिर्णयः, on diagnosing disease. One fabricated citation removes the reason to trust the other twenty-two, so the panel was taken down rather than kept with a disclaimer.

Sāmudrika Śāstra was considered and set aside. It reads the body, not the birth moment, and has no place in a reading produced from birth data.

Coverage

Chapters 10, 16, 19 and 26 are complete for the verses used. Chapters 20 and 21 — the sub-periods of the daśā — are not yet transcribed. The nakṣatra material is pending, and will come from Bṛhat Jātaka chapter 16 rather than from BPHS, since that is where it actually is.
